## [0.4.0](https://github.com/ArcheeNic/lower-vaults/releases/tag/v0.4.0) — 2026-11-25

- **Walking together.** Every run opens a room with a six-character code shown in the upper-right corner. Friends enter it on the title screen with **Join by code** and walk the same expedition, up to eight explorers per room. Fellow walkers carry a glowing shoulder lamp. Sign-in is anonymous through Epic Online Services, no account needed.
- Builds now ship as archives. Extract the whole archive and keep the EOS libraries beside the game.

## [0.3.0](https://github.com/ArcheeNic/lower-vaults/releases/tag/v0.3.0) — 2026-11-25

- **Hard mode.** Darkness gathers along your trail. Stepping back into it drains health, and once health runs out, the dark claims you.
- **Map size.** Normal and hard runs can be played on a small, medium or large map.
- Empty hall platforms are now broken up by partitions and furnished.

## [0.2.0](https://github.com/ArcheeNic/lower-vaults/releases/tag/v0.2.0) — 2026-11-25

- **Normal mode.** Each run assembles a new tower from modular rooms. To get out, find the door with daylight behind it. **Sandbox** keeps the free walk that starts in the Archive of Weights and Measures.
- **Touch of the inhabitants.** A touch now leaves a mark: the Lamplighter puts out the lights, the Diver floods your visor, the Pilgrim returns you to the start, the Spindle spins you around, and there is more.
- **Finds.** The amber seal absorbs one touch. The hourglass drives the inhabitants away for 45 seconds.
- **Living halls.** Machinery works, shoals and swarms drift, steam jets burst. Each room has its own sounds: bells, clocks, tuning forks, water and vents.
- Depth fog now follows the camera's view distance.

## [0.1.0](https://github.com/ArcheeNic/lower-vaults/releases/tag/v0.1.0) — 2026-11-24

- First-person walk through an endless tower: three settings linked by stairways between levels.
- Non-Euclidean passages and local gravity: you can walk up a wall onto the ceiling.
- Inhabitants, each with a sound of its own, and a pursuer.
- Ten interface languages, switchable on the title screen and in the pause menu.
- Standalone builds for Windows and Linux.
